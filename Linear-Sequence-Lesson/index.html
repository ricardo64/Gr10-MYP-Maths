Fernando's Recipe for Sequences

Study Fernando's step-by-step strategy for finding the \(n^{\text{th}}\) term rule.

Step 1: Term Table & Difference
Term Number (\(n\)) 1 2 3 4
Sequence 2 5 8 11

The common difference between each term is +3. Because it goes up by 3 each time, the rule starts with \(3n\).

Step 2: Adjusting to match
\(3n\) sequence 3 6 9 12
Target sequence 2 5 8 11

To get from \(3n\) (\(3, 6, 9, 12\dots\)) to our target sequence, we must subtract 1.

Full Rule: \(3n - 1\)
