What is a Geometric Sequence?

A sequence where each term is found by multiplying the previous term by a constant value called the common ratio (\(r\)).

General Formula
\(a_n = a_1 \times r^{n-1}\)
  • • \(a_n\) = the \(n^{\text{th}}\) term
  • • \(a_1\) = the first term
  • • \(r\) = the common ratio
  • • \(n\) = term position
Example: 2, 6, 18, 54, 162, ...

First term \(a_1 = 2\), Common ratio \(r = 3\)

Rule: \(a_n = 2 \times 3^{n-1}\)
\(a_5 = 2 \times 3^{5-1} = 2 \times 3^4 = 2 \times 81 = 162\)
Question 1

Sequence: 5, 10, 20, 40, ...

Integer Ratio (\(r=2\))

(i) Common ratio & next terms

Common ratio \(r = \frac{10}{5} = 2\)

(ii) General term \(a_n\)

Substitute \(a_1 = 5\) and \(r = 2\)

(iii) 10th term (\(a_{10}\))

Evaluate \(a_{10} = 5 \times 2^9\)

Question 2

Sequence: 81, 27, 9, 3, ...

Fractional Ratio (\(r=\frac{1}{3}\))

(i) Common ratio & next terms

Common ratio \(r = \frac{27}{81} = \frac{1}{3}\)

(ii) General term \(a_n\)

Substitute \(a_1 = 81\) and \(r = \frac{1}{3}\)

(iii) 10th term (\(a_{10}\))

Simplify \(a_{10} = \frac{81}{19683}\)
